Could you run these eight symmetry-policy training jobs on your GPU cluster?

Clone https://github.com/RileyLink/EQTrackingControl using branch
codex/quadrotor-weak-symmetry, then follow CLUSTER_HANDOFF.md in the repository.
It has the Python/CUDA environment setup, a short eight-job smoke test, and
the SLURM array command for the full runs. Your site's GPU partition/account
options may need to be added to the sbatch command.

The eight jobs are full, original strong, corrected strong, and weak modes
for quadrotor and Astrobee. Each trains from scratch with 6 x 128 hidden units.
Budgets are 1 billion quadrotor steps and 400 million Astrobee steps per policy.
The runner checkpoints and automatically resumes interrupted jobs.

After all eight are complete, run the guide's package command and send me BOTH
symmetry-policies-seed0.tar.gz and symmetry-policies-seed0.tar.gz.sha256 through
a download link I can access. It collects the complete learned weights,
configurations, provenance, and training curves automatically. Please keep the
originals until I verify the download.
